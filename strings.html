Performance Requirements
  1. Scales and arpeggios are to be performed from memory, at the tempo indicated and with the bowing shown on the list.
  2. Candidates must not perform the scales and arpeggios over a greater range than stipulated.
  3. Candidates should react promptly to the scale/arpeggio being asked, without the need to ‘practice’ or run through the fingering prior to performing.
  4. Single-note scales are played as quavers. Violin, viola and cello: separate bows, or slurred with one octave to a bow. Double bass: pizzicato, separate bows, or slurred two beats to a bow.
  5. Major and minor arpeggios are played as quavers at the dotted-crotchet tempo. Violin, viola and cello may slur 3 or 9 notes to a bow, at the candidate’s choice. Double bass: pizzicato, separate bows, or slurred one beat to a bow.
  6. Chromatic scales on violin, viola and cello: separate bows, or slurred 6 or 12 notes to a bow at the candidate’s choice. Double bass: pizzicato, or three beats to a bow.
  7. All scales are to start on the tonic and arpeggios are to be played in root position.
  8. Scales and arpeggios are to ascend and descend, starting on the lowest possible key note (unless otherwise indicated).
  9. Dominant 7ths are played in the stipulated key and resolve on to the tonic, with the leading note rising to the tonic. Violin, viola and cello may slur 4 or 8 notes to a bow, at the candidate’s choice. Double bass: pizzicato, separate bows, or slurred two beats to a bow.
  10. Diminished 7ths start on the stipulated note, with the same choice of bowing as dominant 7ths.
  11. Double-stopped scales use the bowing on that line. 6ths are separate bows. Violin and viola octaves are separate bows, and 3rds are slurred with repeated notes. Cello octaves and 3rds are broken steps. Double bass octave scales are separate bows or pizzicato only. Viola and double bass scales and arpeggios are played with even notes.
