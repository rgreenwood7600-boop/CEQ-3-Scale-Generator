Performance Requirements
  1. Scales and arpeggios are to be performed from memory, at the tempo indicated and as per the range and articulation stipulated by the examiner.
  2. Candidates must not perform the scales and arpeggios over a greater range than stipulated.
  3. Candidates should react promptly to the scale/arpeggio being asked, without the need to ‘practice’ or run through the fingering/slide positioning of the scale prior to performing.
  4. Scales to the 12th: Candidates may choose to perform scales and arpeggios stipulated, either to the 12th straight or broken at the 11th.
  5. Major and minor arpeggios are to be played in triplet quavers.
  6. Candidates may take a breath during a scale, but it should not impact on the flow and fluency of the exercise.
  7. All scales are to start on the tonic and arpeggios are to be played in root position.
  8. Scales and arpeggios are to ascend and descend, starting on the lowest possible key note (unless otherwise indicated).
  9. Dominant 7th arpeggios are to be played in a stipulated key and resolve on to the tonic (except pianists).
  10. Diminished 7th arpeggios are to start on a stipulated starting note.
  11. The naming of scales as asked by the examiner applies to notation only: no adjustment should be made by the candidate based on the pitch of the instrument, e.g. C Major on a clarinet will sound B♭ Major at concert pitch.
